NEON RAIDER ARCADE

An arcade-grade retro space shooter featuring 60FPS combat, modular weapon upgrades, alien dreadnoughts, and real-time global leaderboards.

ABOUT THE GAME

Neon Raider Arcade is an action-packed retro space combat simulator playable directly inside your web browser. Take the helm of experimental starfighters, navigate dense asteroid swarms, salvage rare Amethyst Scrap, and destroy advancing alien motherships.

HOW TO PLAY & FLIGHT CONTROLS

Command your vessel using smooth touch gestures on mobile screens, mouse glide on desktop, or standard W-A-S-D and arrow keyboard controls. Firing cycles are automated based on your active weapon type. Collect power-ups and scrap to upgrade kinetic shields, projectile velocity, and attractor magnets.
